## Brand & Style
The design system bridges disciplined high-school academic rigor with the high-dopamine momentum of modern gamified ed-tech platforms. Built to serve secondary school students, Thanaweya Amma candidates, and ambitious language learners, the visual tone eliminates pedagogical dryness in favor of energetic motivation, authority, and relentless forward progress.

The aesthetic fuses **Modern Clean EdTech** with **Tactile Gamification**:
- Deep navy architecture grounds the visual hierarchy in focus, structural trust, and classroom seriousness.
- High-saturation amber and incandescent orange accents inject warmth, urgency, and reward—powering streaks, interactive milestones, and pivotal primary calls-to-action.
- Surfaces maintain crisp airiness through cool slate and off-white bases, preventing cognitive fatigue during extended video sessions and intensive practice drills.
- Micro-interactions communicate tactile feedback: buttons feature subtle bottom-edge bevels, completed tasks trigger vibrant emerald celebration states, and course navigation flows smoothly across dual English/Arabic contextual cues.

## Colors
The color palette uses deliberate role separation to balance sustained visual focus with high-energy milestones:

- **Primary (`#0B192C`):** Midnight Academy Navy. Anchors top-level chrome, navigation sidebars, lesson unit headers, and heavy typography. A secondary shade (`#1E3E62`) handles interactive card borders, secondary containers, and active nav states.
- **Secondary (`#FF9D23`):** Dynamic Amber. Drives primary action prompts, daily streak flames, XP point tallies, and unlock alerts. Pairs with an energetic tertiary punch of Radiant Orange (`#F87A1E`) for hover states, urgency timers, and rank badges.
- **Tertiary (`#10B981`):** Mastered Emerald. Dedicated strictly to affirmative progress: perfect quiz scores, completed modules, correct answer feedback, and certificate verifications.
- **Neutral (`#F8FAFC` & `#F1F5F9`):** Slate White and Canvas Mist. Forms the expansive background foundation, keeping lesson reading contrast comfortable while preventing glare. Dark neutral ink (`#0F172A`) ensures AAA compliance across body copy and translated glossaries.

## Typography
Typography is tuned for bilingual agility and split-second legibility. **Plus Jakarta Sans** provides a friendly, crisp, modern structural shell with open counters and balanced geometry, seamlessly pairing alongside standard modern system Arabic typefaces (such as Cairo or Readex Pro) without visual height mismatch.

**Space Grotesk** serves as the specialized label and gamification voice: timers, question itemizers (`Q 04/20`), streak counters, lesson module codes (`UNIT 03 - GRAMMAR`), and score tickers render with precise technical energy. 

- Keep headline weights decisive (`700` and `800`) to anchor unit titles against saturated badges.
- Standard English grammar rules and vocabulary definitions utilize `body-lg` to prioritize line tracking during intensive reading tasks.
- For dual-language translations, ensure bilingual sub-labels default to `body-sm` with a secondary muted text color (`#475569`).

## Layout & Spacing
The layout leverages a fluid 12-column responsive grid on desktop and tablet, collapsing to a single-column 4-grid view on mobile screens. 

- **Desktop (1024px+):** Sidebar persistence for unit navigation (280px fixed rail), flanked by a fluid content viewport maxed at 1280px. Lesson cards conform to 3-column rows (`span 4`), while active quiz interfaces center within an 8-column reading column (`span 8` with 2 empty boundary columns).
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** Side navigation compresses into an off-canvas drawer or collapsible slim icon bar. Content reflows into a 2-column card orientation with 1.25rem gutters.
- **Mobile (<768px):** Single-column layout. Margin shrinks to `1rem` to maximize screen real estate for interactive grammar exercises, exam questions, and video players. Action controls lock to a fixed sticky bottom shelf.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth mimics physical learning artifacts combined with modern SaaS surfaces:

- **Flat Slate Base:** The primary app background resides on flat `#F8FAFC`, avoiding unnecessary background textures to keep eye strain minimal.
- **Surface Elevation (Cards & Units):** White container backgrounds (`#FFFFFF`) rise against the base using low-contrast outline structural borders (`1px solid #E2E8F0`) reinforced by ultra-soft tinted ambient shadows (`0 4px 20px -2px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.05)`).
- **Tactile Gamification Lift:** Interactive buttons, streak boxes, and selected quiz choices carry an intentional physical baseline: a 2px to 3px solid bottom border offset (`border-b-2` or `border-b-4`) in a darker tone of their base hue (e.g., `#FF9D23` gets a bottom edge of `#D97706`). On active click/press, the element translates down 2px, eliminating the shadow/border gap for authentic tactile feedback.
- **Floating Learning Controls:** The sticky video playback bar, live question submission pill, and floating dictionary use translucent backdrop-filter glassmorphism (`rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.88)` with `blur(12px)`) to stay legible across layered video and course scrolling.

## Shapes
A roundedness factor of `2` strikes the ideal balance between academic professionalism and inviting, friendly motivation:
- Base components, card modules, lesson containers, and video enclosures leverage `0.5rem` (`8px`) to `1rem` (`16px`) corners.
- Modal dialogs, quiz overlays, and unit roadmaps use extra-large radii (`rounded-xl` / `1.5rem` / `24px`).
- Chips, badges, streak tags, and countdown counters consistently use full pill contours (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) to emphasize gamified, tactile micro-objects.

## Components

### Buttons & Action Bars
- **Primary CTA:** Vibrant Amber (`#FF9D23`) with bold navy text (`#0B192C`) and a 3px darker amber bottom edge (`#D97706`). Hover triggers radiant glow and scale lift.
- **Secondary / Review Action:** Midnight Navy background with crisp white text and deep slate hover states.
- **Ghost Action:** Transparent background, `1.5px` border in `#CBD5E1`, dark navy text.
- **Sticky Control Bar:** Bottom dock housing the forward/back progress triggers, audio playback speed, and "Submit Answer" button with full mobile responsiveness.

### Badges & Gamified Streak Indicators
- **Streak Pill:** Saturated `#FFFBEB` background with `#B45309` text, enclosed by a glowing `#F87A1E` flame icon and bold numerical counter in `Space Grotesk`.
- **Grade / Level Badges:** Pill-shaped tags designating vocabulary levels (e.g., `A2`, `B2`, `Thanaweya Final`) with deep navy text encased in translucent colored rings.

### Course Units & Lesson Cards
- **Structure:** Crisp white surfaces framed with `1px` `#E2E8F0` borders. Header includes lesson sequence, estimated completion time, and progress ring.
- **State Variations:**
  - *Locked:* Muted slate surface with lock glyph and minimum XP prerequisite.
  - *Active:* Midnight Navy header badge, vibrant amber progress bar tracking completion percentage.
  - *Mastered:* Emerald checkmark icon, top subtle border in `#10B981`.

### Quiz Test Cards & Interactive Exercises
- **Multiple Choice Options:** Full-width interactive cards with radio indicators. 
  - *Default:* White background, `#E2E8F0` border, hover transforms border to `#1E3E62`.
  - *Selected:* Amber-tinted background (`#FEF3C7`), border `#FF9D23`.
  - *Correct Evaluation:* Bright emerald background tint (`#ECFDF5`), `#10B981` border with animated validation check.
  - *Incorrect Evaluation:* Soft crimson tint (`#FEF2F2`), red border with immediate correction banner.

### Video Lesson Stage
- Integrated 16:9 player framed with midnight navy bezel, contextual vocabulary bookmark tabs, interactive transcript drawer on desktop, and immediate timestamp jump points for grammar breakdown segments.
