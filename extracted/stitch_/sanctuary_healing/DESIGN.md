## Brand & Style

This design system delivers a clinical yet soulful atmosphere tailored for integrative wellness, prophetic medicine (cupping/hijama), and modern physical rehabilitation. The brand merges modern healthcare precision with the calming, sacred hospitality of Islamic holistic wellness. 

### Emotional Tenets
- **Reassurance & Dignity:** Patients seeking relief from acute and chronic pain must instantly feel understood, safe, and cared for.
- **Sterile Modernity meets Organic Warmth:** Move away from intimidating hospital blues and aggressive clinical whites. Infuse medical authority with earthy grounding and restorative herbal cues.
- **Holistic Trust:** Prominently communicate clinical certifications, clean sterile standards, and licensed therapy practitioners without visual noise.

### Style Direction
A refined blend of **Modern Minimalist Healthcare** and **Warm Organic Tactility**:
- Generous, breathable layouts evoking decompression.
- Silky layered surfaces using sand, bone, and mint tints rather than stark voids.
- Subtle gold geometric ornamentation and fine micro-patterns, honoring heritage through quiet discipline rather than ornamentation overload.
- Reassuring, rounded cards and soft micro-interactions that communicate gentleness and therapeutic relief.

## Colors

The palette establishes an equilibrium between healing botanical depth, sterile clarity, and understated prestige:

- **Primary (`#0F5B4B` - Deep Healing Emerald):** Anchor for navigation, hero banners, primary action buttons, and dominant brand elements. Conveys clinical mastery, restorative plant medicine, and timeless longevity.
- **Secondary (`#1B7A66` - Sage Teal):** Mid-tone functional color for interactive active states, secondary buttons, badge outlines, and iconography.
- **Tertiary (`#D4AF37` - Soothing Gold):** Reserved as an accent for clinical excellence ratings, certified trust accreditations, premium therapy markers, and subtle decorative dividing lines.
- **Mint Surface System (`#E6F4F1` & `#F0FAF7`):** Tonal card backgrounds and chip fills that signify cleanliness, hygiene, and calm.
- **Canvas (`#FDFCF9` & `#F9F6F0` - Bone / Warm Sand):** Replaces harsh standard whites with a warm, restorative linen backdrop that softens visual strain.
- **Neutral Dark (`#1F2A28` - Dark Slate Pine):** Delivers optimal readability and contrast without the severe harshness of pure black.

## Typography

Typography acts as an empathetic bridge between medical authority and physical comfort. 

### Bilingual Harmony & Font Selection
- **Arabic Language Instances:** When rendering Arabic text, use **Tajawal** for structural headers and **Cairo** or **Tajawal** for body text. The typography hierarchy scales proportionally 1:1 with the defined English scale, maintaining an open x-height and ample line heights (at least 1.6x) to avoid diacritical clipping and preserve fluid readability in Right-to-Left (RTL) mode.
- **Latin Typography Hierarchy:**
  - **Newsreader (Headings & Display):** Offers an editorial, therapeutic gravitas. It elevates session treatments, therapy diagnoses, and philosophy statements from sterile commodity to high-end holistic medicine.
  - **Plus Jakarta Sans (Body, Labels, Actions):** Crisp geometric shapes softened with humanist terminals. Delivers utmost legibility across mobile booking flows, medical symptom checklists, dosage instructions, and calendar timelines.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout embraces an unhurried, comfortable spatial cadence that respects physical mobile interactions:

### Grid & Responsive Rules
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid layout with dynamic safe-area gutters (`1rem`), standard `1rem` lateral page margin. Interactive booking actions and CTA bars stick to the bottom viewport with an elevated thumb-friendly tap target (minimum height: 48px).
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px):** 8-column layout with `1.5rem` gutters and `2rem` margins. Two-column cards for therapy offerings and practitioner cards.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column layout capped at `1280px` max-width container, surrounded by generous warm-sand canvas margins.

### Rhythmic Discipline
- Spacing follows an 8pt architectural rhythm (`4px`, `8px`, `16px`, `24px`, `40px`).
- Always pair therapy step explanations and intake forms with `space-md` or `space-lg` to prevent dense cognitive overwhelm for patients experiencing pain or anxiety.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses atmospheric lightness and therapeutic warmth rather than artificial, dark dropshadows.

### Elevation Strategy
- **Base Surfaces (`Level 0`):** The soothing sand canvas (`#FDFCF9`).
- **Cards and Containers (`Level 1`):** Pure pristine white surfaces (`#FFFFFF`) or tinted mint planes (`#F0FAF7`), resting atop the sand canvas with an ultra-diffused tint: `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(15, 91, 75, 0.05)`.
- **Floating Modals and Active Pickers (`Level 2`):** Elevated appointment time selectors and interactive symptom locators use gentle lift: `box-shadow: 0 12px 36px -4px rgba(15, 91, 75, 0.09)`.
- **Subtle Organic Borders:** Interactive components and structural cards use hairline borders (`1px solid rgba(27, 122, 102, 0.12)`) to convey clean surgical integrity and structured boundary without visual rigidity.

## Shapes

The geometric identity relies on soft, welcoming contours that communicate gentle touch, bodily rehabilitation, and comfort.

- **Primary Components:** Buttons, standard cards, and input modules use `0.5rem` (`rounded-md` / `roundedness: 2`).
- **Feature Wellness Cards:** Service detail trays, sterile guarantee callouts, and practitioner bios utilize `1rem` (`rounded-lg`).
- **Trust Elements & Tags:** Status pills, single-use sterilized tool markers, and appointment status indicators utilize full circular rounding (`rounded-full`).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA ("احجز استشارة / Book Session"):** Background `#0F5B4B` with crisp `#FFFFFF` text. Height 48px on mobile, padded with `1.5rem` lateral space, corner radius `0.5rem`. Subtle hover/focus brightness shift (`#1B7A66`).
- **Secondary CTA:** Translucent mint fill (`#E6F4F1`) with `#0F5B4B` text and hairline border (`1px solid rgba(15, 91, 75, 0.2)`).
- **Tertiary/Ghost:** Text-only in `#1B7A66` with subtle gold-amber active underline (`#D4AF37`).

### Input Fields & Booking Selectors
- Default background in `#FFFFFF` with warm slate `#1F2A28` text.
- Border in `rgba(27, 122, 102, 0.2)`. Focus border animates to primary emerald `#0F5B4B` with a soft 3px mint halo (`rgba(27, 122, 102, 0.15)`).
- Date and time slot chips feature clear availability status: disabled slots in soft slate wash, available slots in `#FFFFFF` border `#1B7A66`, and selected slots filled in full `#0F5B4B` with white text.

### Cards & Service Cards (Hijama & Physical Therapy)
- Dual-toned hierarchy: Pure white header section paired with a soft `#F0FAF7` mint base for session specs (duration, sterile equipment guarantee, licensed therapist gender option).
- Bordered with ultra-fine `1px solid rgba(15, 91, 75, 0.08)`.

### Trust Badges & Clinical Accreditations
- Compact pill or capsule layout containing a gold-tinted emblem (`#D4AF37`), deep emerald label, and fine border.
- Used to signify single-use surgical suction tools, licensed physical therapy boards, and certified hygienic sterilization protocols.

### Checkboxes & Radio Elements
- Custom 20px controls with generous hit areas (minimum 44x44px for touch). Active fill uses primary `#0F5B4B` centered with an organic rounded checkmark or white core indicator.
